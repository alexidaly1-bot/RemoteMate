# Still to test on a real TV / phone

Tested here: both drivers against fake TVs, the photo reader on sample text, and the remote/editor/setup pages in a browser.
Not tested: anything that needs a real phone, real TV, camera or mic.

## Google TV (Kogan G6)
1. Fresh install → app finds the TV on its own → tap → code → paired.
2. Every button, touchpad, long-press OK, volume readout.
3. Voice: tap mic, say "Bluey". Typing into a TV search box.
4. App buttons: SmartTube (tap + hold), Prime Video, BrowseHere.
5. Turn the TV off and on: reconnects by itself.

## Roku
6. Found on the Wi-Fi, one tap, no code.
7. Buttons, touchpad, hold OK, HDMI 1–3, app buttons.
8. Voice: phone hears you → Roku search opens with the words.
9. If it says "blocked": Roku Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps.

## Phone (Galaxy Z Fold6)
10. Edge bar: swipe open, hold to move, fold/unfold keeps size.
11. Editor on the phone: hold to move, ✕, +, Done. Close and reopen: layout kept.
12. Photo: take a photo of Settings › About. Check it names the brand.
13. Quick settings tile.

## Chromebook
14. Opens as a window. Keyboard shortcuts. IP box works if search finds nothing.
