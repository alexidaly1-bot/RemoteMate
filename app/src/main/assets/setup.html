3Use it

On a Chromebook the remote opens as its own window. Your keyboard works too: arrows to move, Enter = OK, Esc = Back, Space = play/pause, + / − volume, M mute, H home.

  • Open: swipe left on the thin bar at the right edge of the screen (or tap it).
  • Move the bar: hold it, then drag up or down.
  • Close: swipe right on the three dots at the top of the remote, or tap the bar again.
  • Touchpad: swipe to move, tap for OK, hold for a long press.
  • Voice: tap the mic and talk, or hold it while you talk.
  • Change buttons: three dots › Edit buttons. Hold to move, ✕ to remove, + to add.
  • Typing: three dots › Type on TV.

Voice and photos stay on your phone and home Wi-Fi. Nothing is sent to us.
